# Aave USDe first-breach hourly coverage audit

[`aave-usde-first-breach-coverage.mjs`](../../scripts/research/aave-usde-first-breach-coverage.mjs) is a pure offline auditor for a v2 first-breach issue. It does no RPC, database read, write, or alert delivery. It checks whether a caller supplied **one timely recorder success and one exact attached observed snapshot per required hourly slot** from issue to the fixed scorer observation close. This is stricter than the scorer's `<=8h` path-gap rule. A score may be observed while this hourly audit reports missing collection.

## Inputs and clocks

1. Build and retain the exact UTF-8 manifest bytes before its first slot with `buildHourlyCoverageManifest`. It contains the venue, aligned one-hour slot IDs from `startAt` through `endExclusiveAt`, a declared time, and the **different** physical SHA of the separately sealed v2 issue schedule. Supply the **exact v2 schedule bytes** and their externally expected physical SHA. The auditor checks canonical schedule bytes, finds the unique schedule slot containing `issue.issuedAt`, and binds the issue to that slot's fixed amount and horizon arm. The coverage manifest must reference the same schedule SHA. It also rederives every coverage slot ID and rejects a changed or shortened manifest or one that fails to cover the issue through the fixed outcome close. Caller supplied bytes, declaration, and SHA prove internal consistency only, **not preregistration or durable membership**.
2. Pass the sealed v2 issue, explicit audit `asOf`, all recorder attempt receipts for expected slots, and the exact database snapshot rows read for that window. Each receipt gives `slotId`, `status`, `attemptedAt`, and for success `snapshotId` plus `snapshotRowSha256`. Here `attemptedAt` is the **completion time** of the recorder attempt, after the row insert; a separate future receipt field would be needed to attest start time. A success whose attached row was created after `attemptedAt` fails. The row hash is SHA-256 of `JSON.stringify(row)` as returned to this offline function; it is not an authenticated database physical-file hash. A success joins one unique row ID, checks the hash, observed Aave USDe reserve identity and atomic-recorder eligibility, observed time in the slot, creation time, and both times against the fixed cutoff. Successful rows must advance strictly in block number, block time, and local observation time from the issue anchor, and neither a snapshot ID nor a pinned block hash may recur, including the issue anchor ID and hash. The caller must preserve the query result and its own read evidence; this function cannot prove a database query was complete or authentic.
3. Required slots intersect `(issuedAt, targetAt+8h]`. A slot at an exactly aligned closing time is excluded because its next sample would occur after the scorer's observation window. The source cutoff is `targetAt+8h+120s`. Pending slots remain pending until their slot end (or the outcome close for the last partial slot) plus the 120-second creation allowance. A later call does not turn a late row into a timely one.

The audit reports each slot and counts for success, pending, missing receipt, duplicate receipt, skipped fresh, skipped unchanged, read failure, insert failure, explicit missed attempt, mismatch, late attempt, and censorship. It separately lists unexpected receipt IDs, duplicate row IDs, and unattached snapshot IDs. Skips are evidence that a recorder attempted a slot, but they are **not a new hourly first-observation sample**; they fail `hourlyCoverageFromCallerReceipts`. An absent attempt receipt is `missing`, not a quiet observation. Duplicate or unattached rows fail coverage. Valid schedule binding returns `issueScheduleSlotId` and `issueScheduleMembership: 'caller_consistent_only'`. `sourceCompleteness` remains `caller_receipt_evidence_only`, and `prospectiveEligible` remains `false`, even when every caller-provided slot and row matches.

This sidecar does not certify a continuous cash path, holder withdrawal, complete database census, independent first-availability time, likely exit duration, probability, or an alert. A durable, independently witnessed slot schedule and recorder-attempt/DB visibility evidence are still needed before prospective scoring can use it as a completeness gate.
