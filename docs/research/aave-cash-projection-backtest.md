# Aave sampled endpoint cash backtest

Run `node scripts/research/aave-cash-projection-backtest.mjs --market DAI --amount-usd 1000000 --horizon-hours 24` against the frozen local Aave V3 checkpoint. `--as-of-unix` can truncate the retrospective view. Markets are the eight named reserves in the frozen grid; horizons are integer seconds corresponding to 8 hours through 30 days. The adapter requires the complete grid, validates its local hash and frozen cohort digest, and takes the chronological boundary timestamp from the recorded boundary block.

For an eligible anchor with sampled reserve cash at least the selected amount, the point scenario subtracts the nonnegative trailing 18–30 hour decline rate multiplied by the requested horizon, clipped at zero. It abstains when that trailing interval is absent. The outcome is **cash in the first complete sample at or after anchor + H**, no more than eight hours after target. The exact observation lag is retained. This is distinct from whether _any_ intermediate sample fell below the selected amount; both fields appear in individual records.

The existing `labelMarketSeries` defines the train/holdout split and 24-hour boundary purge. A gap over eight hours, ineligible future sample, or a pending/near-end target is censored. Paused or inactive windows retain their measured endpoint cash but are excluded from residual fitting and score denominators; their counts remain visible. In particular, measured cash during a pause is not evidence of withdrawal ability.

An empirical 10th–90th percentile **residual interval** is fitted only from train records with completed endpoints and point scenarios. It needs at least 30 such records. A second interval uses greedily nonoverlapping train windows and needs at least 20 records. Its sensitivity score uses a separately selected nonoverlapping subset of completed holdout endpoints; `holdoutNonoverlapScored` gives that denominator. Either interval is null when its support gate fails. Holdout MAE, signed bias (projected minus actual), interval coverage, and mean interval width are reported with their scored denominator. Excluded reasons, point abstention, target lag, and individual records remain inspectable. The main train interval uses overlapping windows, so nominal percentile levels are descriptive rather than calibrated future coverage.

For the 24-hour DAI / $1 million example on the local frozen checkpoint, the train has 1,085 completed projections and 272 nonoverlapping ones. The train residual band is approximately −$1.90m to +$5.77m. On 463 scored holdout endpoints, point MAE is approximately $2.19m and interval coverage is 86.4%, with mean width approximately $7.60m. These values are specific to that market, amount, horizon, checkpoint and already-seen historical holdout; they are not product forecast performance.

The separate nonoverlap sensitivity scores 116 selected holdout endpoints with the nonoverlap train band: observed coverage is 84.5% at approximately $6.75m mean width. Nonoverlapping time windows still share market regimes; this is a dependence check, not an independent validation cohort.

The authenticated forecast evidence adapter calls this backtest with the exact same market, amount, horizon, source checkpoint, and `asOfAt` as its point scenario. It attaches only an `endpointCashInterval` summary: lower/upper sampled-cash bounds, completed train support, and historical holdout counts and error/coverage metrics. Individual backtest records stay out of that package. Each bound is the point projection plus a train residual quantile, clipped to nonnegative cash. The attachment refuses mismatched identities or as-of times. It marks the interval unavailable when the point scenario is stale or otherwise unavailable, the train has fewer than 30 completed projections, or the residual bounds are invalid. During a historical `--as-of-unix` replay, both the scenario and backtest are truncated at that timestamp; later observations and outcomes cannot enter the attached summary.

The exported pure attachment helper accepts caller-supplied backtest summaries and does not authenticate them. Application readers must use `evaluateForecastEvidence` with the frozen checkpoint. Its historical replay still pins the full frozen source digest and split boundary, so `retrospective_only` is not an online as-of certification.

This research measures sampled reserve cash only. It does not establish a same-holder executable exit, a likely shortage duration, a calibrated probability, a volatility alert, or prospective eligibility. The source is locally hash verified but not independently chain attested. The original historical holdout was already known before this model was built, so the evaluation is exploratory; a newly frozen forward cohort is needed for a prospective claim.
