# Aave USDC common-Q holder study (V3)

## Question

At a fixed Aave V3 USDC reserve, how often does a sampled holder's _read-only_ withdrawal call remain executable at the **same raw USDC amount** after 1, 4, 24, 48, and 168 hours? A repeated absolute amount across public holders gives the support audit a comparable Q cell. The existing V1 dynamic ladder and V2 follow-up records remain immutable and separately verified.

## Prospective issue

V3 follows a fresh, verified V1 Aave USDC issue before its H1 target. The V1 baseline block/hash, original asset, destination and five target deadlines remain frozen. The three Q are 1,000,000, 1,000,000,000, and 100,000,000,000 raw USDC (1, 1,000, and 100,000 USDC). V3 uses the sealed V1 receipt-screened commitments for prospective selection and recovers the selected public EOA from its exact pre-baseline aToken Transfer log. It recomputes the sealed V1 receipt digest and holder commitment, and checks the original two independent RPC origins for receipt, canonical block, finalized baseline, EOA code and pinned aToken balance. It chooses the least previously issued holder, breaking ties by the V1 slot and candidate rank. This is a bounded event-nominated sample, not a representative holder census.

For every Q, the issuer requires a verified two-origin call and seals `success`, `covered_revert`, `inconclusive_covered_revert`, or `holder_insufficient_coverage` with the raw proof. A verified withdrawal success counts even when the raw aToken balance comparator sits just below Q because Aave index rounding can permit the call. A below-Q revert is holder unavailable, and an unclassified covered revert remains inconclusive; neither is counted as venue impairment. Any transport, identity, replay, or origin disagreement aborts the issue. No future state is read during issuance.

The issue stores bounded compressed copies of both exact receipts with byte lengths and SHA-256 for offline replay. Each receipt is limited to 64 KiB and 128 logs; the full issue is limited to 256 KiB and the atomic writer requires at least 1 GiB of free disk. A local receipt or issue-size guard produces a bounded, parent-linked operator diagnostic with eligible commitments and the common Q grid. It is unverified without the omitted witness: the guard trigger is an observation, not a proved size cause or forecast censor. The parent remains eligible for a later verified issue while its V1 freshness window lasts. A failed Q assay likewise produces a typed unverified diagnostic and keeps the parent retryable. The support audit reports diagnostics separately from holder outcomes.

## Future score

Only baseline `success` and confirmed `covered_revert` Q enter future scoring. At each due horizon, two origins must select the same first finalized block at or after the frozen target time, then independently replay the same holder/amount withdrawal at that exact block/hash. The score records continued simulated exit, new covered revert, simulated recovery, continued covered revert, or holder attrition. If the capture window closes without a valid target proof, censoring requires a separate two-origin finalized deadline witness. No score is backfilled from a later quote.

These are simulations of call executability. They do not prove a mined withdrawal, original USDC delivery, a probability for all holders, or a validated exit-duration forecast. The [holder support audit](../../scripts/research/holder-exit-support-audit.mjs) keeps V3 in exact route/destination/asset/Q/horizon cells, excludes inconclusive baselines from impairment/control denominators, and suppresses correlated V1/V2 observations only when the same parent, holder, raw Q and horizon overlap. Repeated episodes and shared market shocks still need independence and holdout validation before a forecast claim.

## Native collection

The local Mac issue lane runs at `:39` under the existing direct-exit lock. The loaded score job has `PUBLIC_AAVE_COMMON_Q_V3_ENABLED=1` and adds a bounded V3 sweep at `:11`, `:31`, and `:51` while preserving V1/V2. The V3 score sweep takes at most two due plans and one independent origin pair per plan within a 50-second internal budget. It prefers the two origins that verified the frozen baseline, falling back to the rotating pair selection when those origins are absent. All direct tick Node calls are capped at 384 MiB. The issue and score LaunchAgents were loaded and their effective environment checked on 2026-10-02 UTC. The sealed V1 eligible-holder census used for ranking is single-origin; the selected holder alone receives V3 two-origin proof, so the sample is neither independently verified as a full census nor representative.

The first native run sealed V3 issue 1 at 2026-10-02 00:39 UTC from V1 issue 63. Its selected holder had verified simulated withdrawal success at all three fixed Q baselines. The sealed record is 46,250 bytes. Its H1 target was 2026-10-02 01:18:40 UTC. After the target block finalized, a bounded two-origin replay sealed H1 score 1 at 01:40 UTC: all three Q remained simulated-withdraw-success. This is one control observation for one sampled holder, not a mined payout, duration estimate, or validated forecast.
