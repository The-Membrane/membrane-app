Where Home points

One route, three answers.

Home is state-dependent. The revenue winners open on a feed of the thing you care about; what you care about depends on what you hold. The veteran ruleset’s V7 (no front door for returning users) and the training-wheels rule (restrict, don’t hide, for new users) resolve to the same routing. Click any solid card — it opens the mock.

Never signed Who are you? ↓ Landing Variant A — the carry explained, scanner → Defend. The only marketing surface. Variant B test sends them to the Builder instead. The front door exists only for people who have never walked through it.
Connected · no position Ready to learn? ↓ Builder The board and the gauntlet. First screen is the product doing something — pump.fun’s lesson. Templates get them moving without reading. Training wheels: restricting new users to the practice range makes them faster at the real thing later — measured, not hoped.
Has a position What happened while I was gone? ↓ Position The whole position: collateral, LTV window, headroom, debt — then the belt, deliveries, encounters, calibration, recap. The feed is the terminal. A veteran’s first second is their own state. The Builder stays one click away as the drill range.
The persistent nav — depth-ordered, left to right
Position Borrow Carry Earn Liquidate Defend Builder

Borrow · Carry · Earn · Liquidate · Defend is the depth ladder — increasing expertise left to right, ending at Defend (risk management through the Disco). Borrow is plain debt: CDT minted to the wallet against wallet collateral, deployed nowhere. Carry is the yield loop split out of it — borrow and route through venues in one motion. Only Liquidate is not mocked yet. The current app’s nav (About · Home · Mint · The Disco · Liquidate) leads with its least-used pages and has Portfolio commented out entirely.

Cross-links that matter
  • Position → Builder: every encounter card gets “drill this scenario” — it opens the gauntlet floor built from that event.
  • Builder → Position: clearing a floor with a live position offers “check your real board against this.”
  • Landing → Terms: the signature’s URI resolves to /terms — already built.
  • Calibration → Training grounds: the “Is it luck?” card is the entry point on both surfaces.
What this replaces

  • CyberpunkHome: pre-migration home component. Replaced by the state routing above.
