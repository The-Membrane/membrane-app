# Venue-capacity prospective extension — preregistration proposal

**Status: proposal only, 2026-09-27. Not approved, enrolled, scheduled, or an alert.** This is a proposed **new study ID and ledger**, not a revision to the frozen Sep 26–Oct 9 Aave Core USDC/USDT calendar pilot. Preserve every v1 date, source SHA, missing day, outcome, and split without replacement or backfill. Its existing 06:00 capture and +24h/+7d follow-up continue under their own rules. The v1 score source fixes 14 dates, two markets, seven development dates, ≥20 independent events, and ≥20 independent controls (`scripts/research/aave-core-prospective-score.mjs:8-20,381-445`); the current protocol and its same-size holder outcomes are recorded in `docs/research/venue-capacity-drivers.md` ("Prospective calendar-control pilot").

## Why an extension is necessary

Fourteen dates × two Core markets = **28 market-days**, with only **7 × 2 = 14 development market-days**. An event and a control are mutually exclusive for a given unit, so ≥20 independent executable-exit events **plus** ≥20 independent controls require **at least 40 disjoint units**. Even if all 28 v1 rows were captured, uncensored, independent, and perfectly classified, they are **12 short**; the development subset is 26 short. Repeated holders, common-market incidents, missing captures, and censors reduce the effective count further. A larger finite extension does **not** promise 20 failures: if fewer occur, report the shortfall and leave the predictive claim off. Neither more repeated reads of one holder nor retrospective additions to v1 solve independence.

## Decisions required before activation

The owner must approve and freeze **(1) exact route registry**, **(2) user-relevant fixed size(s) `q` per route/asset**, **(3) duration and capture cadence**, and **(4) maximum disk, RPC, and operator budget**. None is chosen here. Also approve the product meaning of deterioration per assay and which routes can supply truly independent cohorts. Until these decisions, a separate immutable v2 plan, executable capture schedule, owner consent where applicable, and a dry-run verifier exist **before the first outcome read**, enrollment remains off. No threshold or route is selected after examining v2 outcomes.

## Registry and assays — separate risk sets

Each candidate route receives a versioned entry with chain, venue, asset and decimals, target/source contract addresses, code or proxy implementation identity, ABI/selector, exact asset path, holder-selection rule, `q`, baseline block/hash and first-observed clock, observation cadence, horizon(s), and source receipts. A route enters only after a hash-pinned, finalized same-holder baseline succeeds at its fixed size; retain every screened ineligible/failed candidate and reason, with no replacement after outcomes. Require holder claim/allowance/authorization checks appropriate to the call, canonical block recheck, exact code and asset identity at every read, bounded complete source discovery including quiet ranges, immutable physical SHA files, and a reproducible offline verifier. A code-empty address is not proof of signing control. A scanner's narrow recent recipients or first page is not a holder census.

Predeclare **one assay family per endpoint**; never pool their events or controls to clear a count:

| Family                                                  | Fixed-size outcome at the pinned block                                                                                                                                                                                                         | What it does _not_ prove                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Direct underlying withdrawal, e.g. Aave `Pool.withdraw` | Same-holder, same-asset, same-`q` call success → clean, attributable execution deterioration/revert after a successful baseline; record remaining claim, reserve state, gas and error class.                                                   | Simultaneous exits, key control, a completed transaction, or that any revert was caused by cash. |
| ERC-4626 redemption/withdrawal                          | Same-holder, same-vault shares and fixed asset `q` (or separately frozen share `q`), with `previewWithdraw`, `maxWithdraw`, cooldown/authorization and exact-call evidence; clean attributable deterioration is distinct from share attrition. | A secondary-market exit, cash at a later conversion leg, or equivalence with Aave withdrawal.    |
| Secondary swap quote                                    | Same input asset and fixed `q`, route/pool set and quote function at each finalized block; predeclared signed output deterioration threshold and execution-relevance checks.                                                                   | An executable fill, vault redemption, or holder-withdrawal failure.                              |

Use a **route registry gate** before any signal joins an assay: asset/decimals and call semantics verified against deployed code, route economically feasible at `q`, unambiguous source identities, sufficient archive/live RPC coverage and disk reserve, and a prospective pre-outcome baseline. Mark unresolved or changed deployments unavailable; never silently resize `q` or switch to a more favorable pool/holder after seeing a result. Comparisons across families may be descriptive but are not one validation denominator. The existing nominal Curve quote and single-vault holder pilots stay in their own families (`docs/research/venue-capacity-drivers.md`, "Preregistered predictive-quote study" and "Fixed-holder exit instrument").

## Prospective clock, controls, and scoring

For each predeclared capture slot, seal local capture **start, completion, and first-observed time** plus finalized block number/hash and feature/source physical hashes. Warning lead begins when the complete usable feature set was first locally available—not at the earlier chain block timestamp, publisher date, or a later reconstructed feature. Predeclare horizon per family; preserve the v1 +24h/+7d direct-withdrawal outcomes where applicable and require **≥6 hours real lead** for an actionable precursor. A missed start/finish window is `missing`, not shifted; a complete no-event window is `quiet`, not missing. Keep provider ambiguity, identity drift, insufficient balance/share attrition, paused routes, missed outcome targets, and not-yet-due horizons as distinct censored/pending states. Preserve failures and improvements, not only adverse observations. No late read becomes an advance warning.

Start with same-size, same-holder mechanical exit/quote status as the outcome, not cash headroom or a proposal. Compare each candidate marker against contemporaneously knowable **cash/`q`** and **exact pre-anchor cash slope**, plus route-specific simple persistence/lagged-quality baselines where applicable (`scripts/research/aave-core-prospective-score.mjs:273-327`; `docs/research/venue-capacity-drivers.md`, "Exact pre-B cash-slope control"). Keep peer rates, governance proposal/queue/execution, volatility, flows, and pool state separately timestamped; a proposal, cap permission, or falling cash is not an executable impairment. Both positive and negative capacity changes and quiet controls stay visible.

Freeze a chronological development/holdout cutoff **before v2 enrollment**. Apply an outcome-horizon embargo on both sides of the cutoff (at least the longest scored horizon), exclude overlapping target windows from independent counts, and cluster repeated holders, vaults, markets, shared transactions, and common calendar shocks. Define an independent event/control as a disjoint causal episode or matched quiet exposure, not a call, wallet, day, or repeated alert by itself. Score per assay and route, report raw rows and effective independent groups, selected threshold, recall, precision, false notices per venue-week, real-lead distribution, missing/censor counts, and uncertainty. An untouched chronological holdout must show **≥20 independent executable events and ≥20 independent controls in the assay being promoted**, ≥6h real lead, holdout precision at least **10 percentage points above** the best simple baseline, and a bootstrap 95% improvement interval wholly above zero; timestamped governance/volatility classes additionally need ≥5 independent exposures. These are minimum gates from the existing research protocol (`docs/research/venue-capacity-drivers.md`, "Preregistered predictive-quote study"), not a claim that a finite run will achieve them. Report any gate's non-identifiability or failure; no user-facing predictive badge/alert from a proxy or underpowered count.

## Resource and stop rules

Before activation, freeze per-route RPC calls/bytes, maximum chunks, disk reserve (never below the existing 1 GiB collector floor), retry/backoff, and the total observation budget; stop a collector when any bound, source identity, finality, verification, or local capture window fails. Keep the last verified ledger and mark the slot missing/censored; never loosen the bound, overwrite a seal, backfill an earlier warning, or tune a threshold to rescue a sparse result. Review pilot feasibility and attrition at a predeclared checkpoint **without unsealing holdout outcomes for feature selection**. Stop the extension when its approved duration/budget is exhausted even if fewer than 20 events occurred, and publish that as an inconclusive or negative result. The v1 14-day ledger remains immutable regardless of v2 approval or outcome.
