# Aave sampled cash recovery landmark backtest

Run `node scripts/research/aave-cash-recovery-backtest.mjs --market DAI --amount-usd 1000000 --horizon-hours 24` against the frozen local eight-reserve grid. Optional `--elapsed-hours E` examines a single still-below sampled landmark per episode; `--as-of-unix T` truncates source observations. The horizon is flexible from 8 hours through 30 days. This is an offline research study, not a live forecast.

The adapter validates the checkpoint's physical data hash and pinned source entries SHA, requires all eight complete 1,569-sample grids, and derives the split boundary from the frozen boundary block. The exported `evaluateRecoverySeries` is a pure fixture/replay seam and does **not** authenticate caller rows. The source is locally hash verified, not independently chain attested.

An episode begins at the first sampled reserve cash observation below the chosen dollar threshold after a sampled above-threshold observation. Episodes with no observed prior above-threshold sample are left censored and remain in counts. Gaps over eight hours, ineligible samples, and series edges are explicit censor reasons. The optional elapsed landmark is the first still-below observation at or after the requested elapsed time; an episode recovered or censored before then does not reenter the risk set. There is at most one record per episode, rather than one correlated record per below-threshold sample.

The outcome is based on the first sampled above-threshold recovery after the landmark. Its possible crossing time is bracketed `(last below, first above]`. Recovery is definite by `landmark + H` when the first above sample is at or before H. A below sample at or shortly after H is a definite **sampled** negative. A bracket straddling H is unresolved, not forced into either outcome. Ineligible/paused observations, gaps, edges, and a historical as-of before completion stay out of scoring. This does not establish continuous time below threshold: reserve cash may fluctuate between samples.

Chronological train episodes must have their target plus the eight-hour observation allowance before the fixed boundary's 24-hour purge. Train recovery brackets and censor reasons are derived only from rows at or before that cutoff, even if the same episode later recovers. Holdout episodes must **begin** after the boundary's 24-hour purge, so one shortage spanning the boundary cannot enter both sides. A selected series truncated at `asOfAt` cannot score a target whose observation allowance would extend past that time. Counts and censor reasons remain visible for both splits and the excluded boundary area.

An empirical train recovery fraction and exploratory holdout Brier/calibration-gap metrics are exposed only if each split has at least 30/20 distinct scored episodes respectively, with at least five recovered and five unrecovered episodes in each. Distinct episodes can still be correlated across time. Otherwise all estimates are null and `eligible=false`, with exact support failures. Even if a later dataset passes those minimums, complete-case rates may be distorted by informative censoring; they are research diagnostics, not calibrated probabilities. The original frozen holdout was examined before this method existed and is not blind validation.

At `$1m` per reserve, the actual frozen grid has only a few shortage episodes per reserve. The DAI example returns support failure and no empirical recovery fraction or holdout score. No likely duration, holder exit probability, or alert is derived. A prospective, independently captured cohort of many more independent episodes and a holder-specific executable-exit study would be needed before user-facing forecasting.

The offline forecast evidence wrapper (`node scripts/research/aave-forecast-evidence.mjs --market DAI --amount-usd 1000000 --horizon-hours 24`) runs this study at elapsed time zero with the exact market, threshold, horizon, and `asOfAt` used for its existing sampled-cash scenario and endpoint interval. It authenticates the frozen checkpoint before evaluating either study. Its `sampledCashRecoveryLandmark` field carries only aggregate train/holdout episode counts, scored outcome counts, censor/status counts, support failures, and boundary-exclusion counts. It omits episode records, recovery brackets, empirical recovery fractions, and holdout scores. Sparse support yields `status: "unavailable"`; any eventual supported result would remain `retrospective_only`. The field never contains a numeric likely duration or enables an alert.

The exported `attachRecoveryLandmarkEvidence` is a pure replay seam. It requires matching market, amount, horizon, as-of, and nonempty source entries SHA plus the exact study version and zero-elapsed landmark. Those checks prevent accidental study joins, but caller-supplied objects are not authenticated by the seam. Use `evaluateForecastEvidence` for verified local source provenance. As-of replay excludes later observations from the recovery study; it does not make the original examined holdout blind or the result prospective.
