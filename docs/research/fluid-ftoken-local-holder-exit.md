# Fluid fToken prospective holder exit lane

The local Fluid holder recorder covers the three frozen August Carry routes: USDC → Fluid USD Coin [USDC], USDT → fToken [USDT], and GHO → fToken [GHO]. Each uses its exact fToken and underlying asset. Its question is whether a screened holder's exact underlying amount remains callable at a later finalized block. It does not infer a mined payout from `eth_call`; the [historical three-route payout proof](./fluid-ftoken-holder-payout.md) is separate.

## Issue and score contract

An hourly issue tick screens one 4,096-block share-transfer window per route, rotating across 64 windows (about 36 days at Ethereum's usual block cadence). The wider search is needed for thinly traded GHO shares; it does not relax the current EOA/share/assay checks or assert an eligible holder exists. The broad Ankr log read only nominates addresses. A 64-address screening cap records `scan_budget_exhausted` and resumes within the same window on the next tick. A chosen transfer is re-queried in one block on Alchemy and Ankr; the selected raw log, both mined transaction receipts, and selected block hashes are stored and replayed before an issue is sealed. Both origins then verify the same finalized block/hash, direct fToken runtime and Liquidity code hashes, frozen asset and Liquidity address, empty EIP-1967 implementation/beacon slots, underlying/share decimals, empty holder runtime (`undefined` or `0x` from viem), positive shares/claim, and each `withdraw(assets,holder,holder)` simulation. The fixed Q ladder is 1%, 10%, 25%, 50%, and 100% of the holder's `previewRedeem(balanceOf(holder))` raw underlying amount, with zero and duplicate raw amounts removed. The `withdraw` return is **shares burned**, while Q is **underlying assets requested**.

An issue freezes H1/H4/H24/H48/H168 target clocks, each with a two-hour capture deadline. A score chooses the first finalized block at or after the target, verifies its parent boundary on two origins, and rechecks route implementation/liquidity identity. A changed identity is a distinct regime censor. At unchanged identity, the score rereads the _same holder's_ shares and exact Q entitlement before calling `withdraw`; disposed or insufficient shares are holder ineligibility, not a venue revert. A missed window is sealed only after a two-origin finalized head is later than the deadline. Scores contain covered simulation success/revert, holder ineligibility, entitlement unavailable, identity change, or capture-window censor; they never claim delivered underlying tokens. The issue, score, and native attempt ledgers are separate SHA-linked, bounded, fsynced, no-overwrite local chains with a 1 GiB disk reserve. `readPublicFluidHolderEvidence(routeKey,destination)` returns only coarse horizon counts, no holder address, Q, selected transaction, or target clock, and always `calibratedForecast:false`.

## Current state and operations

At first local run on 2026-10-01, the issue tick sealed three `no_fresh_holder` attempts. Review found that the EOA screen wrongly rejected viem's `undefined` empty-code result. After correction, the issue lane found eligible holders on all three routes. At the 2026-10-01 10:00 UTC replay, its offline ledger accepted **3 USDC, 3 USDT, and 2 GHO issues**, each with five fixed Q cases. Both origins finalized past the first USDC and USDT H1 targets, and the scorer sealed **one on-time H1 episode on each route**: five covered simulated successes per episode; the other issued H1 cells remained pending. GHO had no due H1 score yet. These are correlated Q cases, not ten independent holder successes, mined payouts, or a calibrated forecast. Earlier no-holder attempts describe the old screen and must not be interpreted as evidence that no EOA held fTokens. A no-holder or scan-budget attempt is not an independent holder episode or forecast. The Mac issue and score jobs are loaded as `com.membrane.carry-fluid-ftoken-holder-issue` (hourly minute 38) and `com.membrane.carry-fluid-ftoken-holder-score` (minutes 08/18/28/48/58). The native tick takes a nonblocking lock and has a 150-second inner timeout. Each route's candidate scan is one window per tick, at most 64 screened addresses and at most 4,096 raw logs; a failed route records a failed attempt and makes the job exit nonzero.

Run `node scripts/research/carry-fluid-ftoken-holder.mjs verify` to replay local chains and counts. Run `node --test scripts/research/carry-fluid-ftoken-holder.test.mjs` for sizing, future entitlement, EOA, scan cursor, selected receipt, and immutable-ledger regressions. All three routes need later same-holder scores. Numerical exit probability or likely duration requires independent impairment/recovery episodes and holdout validation.
