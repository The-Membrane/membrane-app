# Saturn pending-ticket gate series

The frozen 146-ticket queue cohort has 40 tickets pending at its historical cutoff. Their original minimum prices are not immutable: ticket 1659 has a mined owner update. The two saved cross-sections at blocks 26,107,302 and 26,109,183 show all 40 still below their respective net USDat/share minimums. Two cross-sections with no crossing cannot estimate a likely recovery or final AUSD payment time.

`scripts/research/saturn-queue-pending-series.mjs` adds a local append-only series for those exact 40 IDs. Each observation reuses the current reader's finalized, two-origin contract and cohort checks, then records mutable request status, minimum, shares, original holder, owner, and net USDat quote. Files are numbered and SHA-linked to the verified October 3 snapshot. Offline verification checks every link, ordered ID, source hash, arithmetic, increasing finalized block/time, and terminal-to-requested reversals. The SHA chain detects accidental corruption; it is not an independent external attestation. A terminal ticket is censored from later price-gate comparisons rather than treated as an available AUSD payout.

The existing single `holder-exit-campaign` dispatcher has a `saturn_pending_series` lane. At 1–3 GiB free it alternates the slot-3 ApyUSD and Saturn attempts; at 3 GiB or more it samples Saturn at most daily in slot 5, including while the sUSDe archive is filling. Morpho scoring slots remain unchanged. The 1 GiB plus 256 KiB campaign floor, 1 GiB artifact reserve, 384 MiB Node heap, and 540-second child timeout apply. The wrapper loads `tsx` for its TypeScript dependencies; its `--verify` mode runs offline. No new Mac background item was added.

The Carry forceability reader uses the latest verified series sample when one exists. Its price-gate row keeps the exact cohort, block, and right-censored wait context. A second compact row compares consecutive sampled blocks: tickets that newly lost quote eligibility, still-gated tickets whose quote-to-minimum deficit deepened, and tickets that became quote eligible. Deterioration is an in-app cohort status with source UTC time; it does not claim the selected user's ticket changed. A ticket that leaves Requested is excluded from this price comparison; that transition alone is not evidence of holder payment. With zero series samples, the existing October 3 snapshot remains the displayed observation and no change row appears.

As of October 3, the series has **zero** post-baseline samples: the Data volume is below 1 GiB and no Membrane campaign job is loaded. `--verify` succeeds offline and reports baseline block 26,109,183 with 40 price-gated tickets. `--capture` must wait for the disk guard and the existing campaign to be restored; it has not been run in this work.

Even after samples accumulate, a price-gate crossing only establishes quote eligibility at a sampled block. A likely crossing duration needs enough crossing and non-crossing episodes for a chronological holdout. Operator processing, same-holder USDat transfer, both conversion legs, and final holder AUSD transfer require separate evidence. Do not turn this series into a holder-executable AUSD forecast by itself.
