The route is the trade

Every one of these is someone borrowing on Aave, Spark, Morpho or Compound and putting it somewhere. Borrow rate and venue yield both read from chain. The spread between two rows is far larger than the spread between two protocols — which is why the route, not the venue you borrowed from, is the decision. These routes are the root system: every inefficiency closed here feeds the core the rest of the network grows from.
