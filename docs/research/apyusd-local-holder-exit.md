# ApyUSD local prospective holder observations

The frozen route is `apxUSD → ApyUSD [apxUSD]`: vault `0x38eeb52f0771140d10c4e9a9a72349a329fe8a6a`, apxUSD `0x98a878b1cd98131b271883b390f68d2c90674665`, receipt contract `0x9bf51f33955ec70f87c4b5c49441815589043237`. The current route adapter pins both EIP-1967 implementations and code hashes. The local prospective lane checks those identities on two distinct finalized Ethereum RPC origins before issuing a case or scoring a future case.

One issue freezes a EOA holder selected from recent vault share Transfer logs and six original apxUSD amounts. The issue records two-origin same-holder `withdrawForReceipt(Q, holder, holder)` simulations at a finalized baseline, then fixes H1/H4/H24/H48/H168 observations. A success is **receipt initiation only**. The returned token ID is simulated; `eth_call` mints no receipt. The final apxUSD payout is separately typed `unmeasured_no_mined_receipt_in_study`. A future claimability or final-payout forecast needs an actual on-chain receipt owned by the holder, independent `claim` checks, and settled Transfer receipt evidence.

The holder check now accepts an optional existing receipt ID. When that ID is owned by the entered holder, the pinned `claim(tokenId, holder)` simulation returns a positive apxUSD amount, and two independent RPC origins agree, the app shows a separate simulated receipt claim. It uses the claim call's returned amount, not `previewClaim`; the new withdrawal Q's final payout remains unassessed. This check proves neither signing authority nor mined payment.

The private SHA-linked issue, score and attempt ledgers live under `data/research/venue-signals/carry-public-apyusd-exit-*`. The public holder-evidence API verifies the issue and score chains and returns coarse route/horizon initiation counts through `receiptInitiationEvidence`; generic exit `cells` stay empty so initiation cannot inflate a final exit forecast. The scorer follows every non-omitted fixed Q at the first finalized block crossing the target, including baseline reverts as a separate cohort. Two origins must agree on the target header, proxy identity and `eth_call` result. A missed capture window is sealed only after both finalized heads have crossed the deadline. Network errors are retries, never measured reverts. A no-fresh-holder issue run leaves a private attempt receipt rather than reissuing the same address.

The first live issue was sealed on October 1, 2026 with six baseline initiation successes, zero future scores and zero on-chain receipt claim payouts. This single holder episode does not support a probability, likely duration, or settled exit prediction.

Run `scripts/carry-public-apyusd-exit-tick.sh issue|score` locally. The native templates schedule issue at minute 24 and score at minutes 7/17/27/37/47/57. The wrapper uses one nonblocking lock if an issue and score ever overlap. `--verify` on each research script checks its private chain. No Neon connection is used.

Two separate local evidence paths are ready but have **zero captured rows** while the disk guard holds. `scripts/research/apyusd-open-receipt-continuity.mjs --capture` links repeated two-origin observations of those same seven receipts. `scripts/research/apyusd-open-receipt-postcutoff-transfers.mjs --tick` scans from block 26,107,207 in contiguous finalized windows, comparing Ankr and Infura Transfer logs, event block headers, and pinned `ownerOf` states for all seven IDs. It permits at most one 1,000-block window by default, two if explicitly called programmatically, five event-block headers, 80 RPC calls, and 150 seconds. Both commands enforce the 1 GiB disk reserve before RPC or writing; `--verify` is offline. A transfer or missing `ownerOf` result remains payout-unverified. Final apxUSD payment requires a separately attested mined burn transaction and same-transaction apxUSD transfer to the holder.

The post-cutoff [mined payout join](../../scripts/research/apyusd-open-receipt-postcutoff-payouts.mjs) is implemented for those seven fixed IDs. `--capture ID` requires a contiguous scanned burn by the original holder, a successful mined transaction with exactly one receipt burn and one positive apxUSD transfer from the receipt contract to that holder, two agreeing RPC origins, and a fresh canonical burn-block check. It binds the original gross request and both block times to the verified archive and scan, writes only after those checks, and caps the read at 48 RPC requests or 120 seconds. `--verify ID` repeats the live two-origin mined read and compares it with the saved proof; local SHA and witness checks alone establish only internal consistency. The scan has no captured rows, so no post-cutoff payout proof or duration has been created.

The verified historical 96-receipt cohort has 89 mined same-receipt holder payouts and seven open receipts. Keeping all 96 in the denominator, 16 had paid by day 7, 74 by day 21, and 87 by day 28; all seven open receipts had already exceeded 28 days by the last paid transaction's timestamp. The censor-aware median request-to-payout time is 20.037 days. This is holder claim timing after eligibility, not time unable to exit: all 96 exact-holder claim simulations succeeded at the first eligible block after the same 72-hour gate. Its short enrollment window supplies no mature longer-horizon chronological training set for a validated forecast.
The app's forceability card now shows this timing as **past request→paid** beside the 72-hour historical claim gate, separate from current claim simulations and prospective exit predictions.

The [chronological as-of diagnostic](../../scripts/research/apyusd-receipt-timing-backtest.mjs) replays the verified cohort without using payments learned after the first later request to train on the early half. At the 48/48 split on August 27, 16:49:35 UTC, only **3 of 48** early receipts had a known payout; for a seven-day outcome, 3 were known paid, **0 known unpaid**, and **45 censored**. The later half has 9 paid within seven days and 39 not, as observed through the saved October 1 payout cutoff. Training on the early half's eventual 44 payouts would leak future events into the model. Three holders also occur in both halves. This data cannot calibrate a seven-day payment likelihood as of enrollment, and payment time remains different from the 72-hour ability-to-claim boundary.
