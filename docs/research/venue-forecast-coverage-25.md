## Rulings for a new or sparse venue

1. **Freeze the object.** Register chain, contract/proxy/code version, route graph (every vault, wrapper, pool, bridge and output asset), token decimals, holder-selection rule, fixed amount `q`, horizon `H`, and exact outcome assay. A changed route or asset is a new version. Query the same holder at the same `q` for an exit-ability outcome; route cash, swap quote and actual payout remain separate fields.
2. **Apply mechanical bounds first.** Read cooldown and queue times, outstanding claims, caps, utilization, paused states, slashing, allocation limits, redemption fees, and each conversion leg at one finalized block. A hard lock sets an earliest possible exit; a quota or cash balance supplies an upper bound only after ownership and competing claims are accounted for. Check a holder's exact amount directly: a vault getter may report zero while a smaller same-holder withdrawal call succeeds, as [observed for two AUSD VaultV2 holders](../../data/research/venue-signals/local-morpho-ausd-threshold-v1/2026-10-02-issue66-67-baseline-h1.json) at baseline and H1. In that case, do not treat `maxWithdraw` or idle vault cash as the holder's executable limit. An independently bound full-route final-asset simulation supports a distinct `conditional_projection` tier without prospective validation or mined delivery. Every required stage, conversion execution, exact holder/Q, source and decimals remains mandatory; first-leg simulations, getters and quotes remain stage-only. The mined-receipt tier and its eligibility gate stay separate.
3. **Separate gross demand from replenishment.** Collect contiguous intervals, including verified quiet ranges, for gross holder withdrawals, deposits/repayments, route swaps, internal transfers and resulting endpoint inventory. Reconcile events to balance changes under a route-specific accounting identity. `net = replenishment − gross depletion` is useful context but cannot estimate how many users can leave. An observed maximum is labelled by window/coverage and never offered as a guarantee. Historical or assumed gross-flow means/ranges may support labeled conditional estimates; missing coverage and extrapolation assumptions remain explicit.
4. **Use analogs narrowly.** An analog may provide a prior _scenario_ only when route mechanics, outcome metric, output asset/unit, holder constraints, and horizon align. Compare normalized gross flow or inventory change relative to the relevant starting inventory and apply the result only within verified current mechanical capacity bounds; absolute old dollar deltas do not automatically scale to a new reserve level. Reject shifts in utilization, asset peg, cap, queue, incentives, code or market regime that make the source venue/period incomparable. Exclude shared incident windows; use leave-one-venue-out evaluation so the target venue contributes no training examples. Widen an analog interval for venue mismatch and missing legs; if no out-of-venue holdout passes, retain labeled conditional analog scenarios and estimated flow with uncertainty and assumptions, while withholding calibrated probability or a supported predictive claim. Never borrow holder success from aggregate cash or pool inventory, or pool independent-looking samples that share a vault, holder, pool, governance event or market shock.
5. **Separate conditional and calibrated claims.** Verified historical cash/inventory distributions and hash-pinned exact-holder assays may support historical backtests and educated conditional projections now, with window count, dates, simulation/payment status, source assurance, errors and assumptions. They need not wait for prospective validation or mined payout. To promote its band to a forward calibrated claim, require at least 20 physically disjoint exact-subject pairs in **each** of fit, calibration and untouched chronological holdout, a horizon embargo, ≥80% realized holdout band coverage and useful sharpness against a persistence band. A supported calibrated point estimate must also beat persistence on holdout MAE; an assumption-labeled conditional point estimate reports its backtest error without claiming that gate passed. An arbitrary user `q`/`H` cell is not validated merely because another cell passes. For a predictive holder alert, require ≥20 independent executable impairments and ≥20 independent controls on untouched holdout, ≥6 hours actual lead, precision ≥10 percentage points above the strongest simple baseline and bootstrap 95% improvement wholly above zero. Censor/missing counts and false notices per venue-week accompany any promoted cell.
6. **Score recovery separately.** Start a duration episode only after an observed fixed-`q` impairment. Track first sampled recovery interval, still-impaired and right-censored cases; do not use the length of one past run as “likely duration.” Historical duration scenarios may be projected conditionally with explicit regime assumptions, observed crossing brackets and censor/open counts. Treat 20 independent recoveries and 20 still-impaired/control episodes on untouched holdout as a minimum screening floor for supported calibrated duration claims, not sufficient evidence by itself. Those claims also need calibrated coverage and uncertainty for each horizon. Keep elapsed restriction, mechanical earliest release, claim eligibility and operator payment delay separate; unknown queue service or pause release has no guaranteed end.
7. **Treat news as a timestamped fact.** Record proposal, vote, execution, pause, exploit, redemption-terms change or relevant market event with original source, first-known time and affected route. Show contemporaneous observed inventory movement and the user's amount as separate facts. A news item is a volatility _candidate_. Explicit parameter-change or event scenarios may estimate conditional timing/flow impacts now, with source, assumptions and affected route; a headline alone establishes neither authority nor causality. Promote causal/calibrated predictive claims only after the outcome and false-notice gates pass.

For a user amount, choose one accounting path. A net-inventory projection already embeds depletion and replenishment: subtract user Q once, without subtracting competing flow again. A separate gross-flow projection starts from current cash, adds estimated replenishment and subtracts estimated gross depletion and user Q once. Shared claims and route bottlenecks can reduce either proxy. Missing gross flow may leave a labeled net-cash conditional scenario available; missing holder or final-route execution proof prevents a full holder-exit claim. Report estimated means/ranges with coverage and assumptions rather than treating displayed capacity as wholly available to the user.
