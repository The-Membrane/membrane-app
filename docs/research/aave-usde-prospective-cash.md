# Aave V3 USDe sampled-cash prospective pilot

**Predeclared design, 2026-09-28. No live issues or outcomes yet.** This pilot tests a sampled reserve-cash proxy, not a holder's executable withdrawal, an exit duration, or a probability of future failure. A forecast of cash below a requested size does not imply that a holder can or cannot withdraw.

## Fixed score grid

Issue and eventually score only the nine predeclared cells: `q = $1m, $10m, $50m` crossed with `H = 8h, 24h, 7d`. A caller may ask for another positive `q` and an integer `H` from 8h through 30d as a **descriptive scenario**, but it must never enter this pilot's score denominator. Do not select cells after looking at outcomes. The two arms are (1) persistence of the anchor's sampled USDe reserve cash, and (2) a linear continuation of the exact observed pre-anchor cash decline, clipped at zero. Both are unavailable if the trailing interval is incomplete. The slope arm uses no fitted coefficient. A fixed-grid issue with anchor cash already below `q` remains in the ledger as `preexisting_shortage`; it is outside the clean `at_risk` onset set and cannot count as a warning hit or lead time even if future cash remains below `q`.

## Eligible issue

The source is an enabled `aave-v3-usde` `venue_snapshots` row with `source='observed'`, Ethereum mainnet, `params.kind='atoken-liquidity'`, a finalized and hash-pinned block, and matching configured/on-chain aToken, underlying, and decimals. Its raw `underlyingBalance` is a nonnegative integer; `instant_usd` must agree with raw balance divided by `10^decimals` under the recorder's explicitly recorded **$1/USDe display assumption**. That assumption is not a market price. Require one strictly increasing sequence of block numbers, block times, and first-local-observed times; no reused block/hash or duplicate local time. The local `created_at` must also be known by issue time and within two minutes of `observed_at`. The issue time follows its anchor's first local observation by at most 10 minutes. The finalized block time may be at most two hours old. Any backfilled source, later-discovered old block, stale/late anchor, identity change, or chronology failure is ineligible. The row records whether it came from `recorder_atomic_v1`; the current hourly `recorder-tick.sh` still invokes the legacy insert path, so requiring that marker would make all current USDe receipts ineligible. Atomic provenance remains visible for later stratification, rather than silently assumed.

At issue, consider only qualifying samples first seen no later than issue time. Choose the prior sample nearest 24h before anchor within 18–30h, with at most 8h between every consecutive sample in that interval. Require the same source identity and verified provenance throughout. If no such complete interval exists, retain the baseline cash and mark both scenarios `unavailable` with a reason. Do not substitute historical archive rows after issue.

Each issued receipt binds its source row ID, block/hash/time, local observation and issue times, anchor age at issue, identity, raw baseline, `q/H`, the canonical ordered path of known trailing source row IDs/block hashes/local times/raw cash, chosen pre-anchor row, exact slope inputs, scenarios, and a SHA-256 of canonical receipt contents. This binds the **observed path presented to issuance**, including interior samples, but does not by itself prove that the DB query returned every eligible row. An implementation that persists receipts must use insert-once semantics. This module performs no DB or RPC access.

## Outcome and denominator

Target time is **`issuedAt + H`**, so an anchor observed up to 10 minutes before issuance never shortens the caller's requested future horizon. At score time, choose **the first** newly observed, same-identity, finalized, hash-pinned sample at or after target and no later than target + 8h. Require an unbroken prospective sample path from anchor to target with gaps no greater than 8h. A missing target, excessive gap, invalid or identity-drifted sample, or source sample first observed/created after target + 8h is retained as a named censored/missing outcome; it cannot silently disappear from the denominator. **A delayed scoring job does not erase a timely source sample**: record its delay beyond the target window separately. A pre-window-close `pending` response is unsealed and must not be persisted as a final score. The target label is sampled cash below `q`, not a continuous crossing or an executable exit. Final score receipt binds issue SHA and chosen outcome row SHA; an issued or scored receipt is never rewritten or rescored. The score function requires an explicit existing-score lookup result, and the eventual storage layer must enforce insert-once by issue SHA. An outcome first seen or created after score time cannot enter that score. False warnings, misses, abstentions, target lag, scoring delay and censoring must be reported separately for each cell when the pilot has data. Overlapping hourly anchors are correlated; episode-grouped results are required before any calibration claim.

No live capture, migration, notification, or production forecast is authorized by this file. The current local disk reserve still prevents some live acquisition; the pure module and synthetic tests only establish the prospective rules.

## Explicit research runner

`node scripts/research/run-aave-usde-cash-ledger.mjs --issue` issues the fixed grid from the current observed USDe snapshots; `--score` scans due issues. Both commands are manual, require at least 1 GiB free disk, and read the exact enabled `aave-v3-usde` entry in `tools/venue-recorder.config.json`. Export `CASH_PUBLISHER_DATABASE_URL` for a separately provisioned, non-owner publisher role with only the audited read and function grants. The runner rejects a URL identical to `DATABASE_URL` or `DATABASE_URL_UNPOOLED`; the store also audits effective PostgreSQL privileges before each transaction. It uses a session-capable Neon Pool, not an HTTP query transaction. No migration or live run has been performed.

Output is one JSON status object, including fixed-grid arms or per-issue score attempts; database and connection errors are reduced to a fixed failure code so credentials do not reach logs. The issue attempt tables retain scheduled and terminal arm receipts for an invocation that reached the database. **Manual invocations alone do not prove that every expected issuance time occurred.** An independently defined expected-slot manifest and reconciliation are required before claiming schedule completeness or using the pilot as calibrated prospective evidence. No runner output represents holder-executable exit ability or exit duration.
