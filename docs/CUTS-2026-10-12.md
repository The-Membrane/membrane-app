### Restoring a single cut

- Re-checked after the rebase and cut 13. These shas revert cleanly on their own at
  the tip: **3, 6, 9, 10, 12, 13**.
- The rest delete entries from the same shared lists as a later cut: robots.txt
  Disallow lines, Layout `EXECUTABLE_ROUTES`, HorizontalNav arrays, middleware
  `TOP_LEVEL_ROUTES`, and the ditto-gating mirror. A standalone `git revert <sha>`
  on one of these hits a small conflict in those files (cut 1: ditto-gating; 2:
  HorizontalNav, Layout, ditto-gating; 4 and 5: robots.txt; 7: Layout, ditto-gating,
  next.config; 8: ditto-gating; 11: middleware, robots.txt). To resolve, keep the
  current list and add back the one route line the cut removed. The deleted files
  themselves never conflict. Cut 7's next.config conflict comes from `26b0d525`;
  revert it first (`git revert --no-commit 26b0d525 f8a77628`) and only the Layout
  and ditto-gating conflicts remain.
- If you only need the files back: `git checkout a34e4f9a -- <paths from the Files removed column>`, then add back the ref lines by hand.
- To restore everything: from the branch tip, `git revert --no-commit cee62d63^..HEAD`.
  It reverts, newest-first, all 13 cuts plus the commits between them (both ledger
  commits and `26b0d525`), and anything committed on top later. Verified after the
  rebase: conflict-free, and the tree is identical to `a34e4f9a` (`git diff --stat
  a34e4f9a` is empty). Do not end the range at the last cut. `cee62d63^..55d3e7e7`
  leaves out the ledger commit that follows cut 13, so reverting `f8bc42e4` (which
  added this file) hits a modify/delete conflict here and the revert stops partway.
- To restore a contiguous tail of cuts, run `git revert --no-commit <sha>^..HEAD` with
  the oldest cut you want back. Each of the 13 tails was checked: conflict-free, with a
  tree identical to that cut's parent. This also avoids the list conflicts above. A
  tail also reverts the ledger commits it spans.

No permanent redirects were added for the cut routes. A 308 is cached by browsers,
so it would keep shadowing a route after a revert. The routes now 404, either through
middleware (top-level routes) or because no page matches.
