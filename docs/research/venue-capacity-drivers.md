## Instrument first

- Aave V3 USDe: `instant_usd` is underlying USDe held by its aToken, at the recorder's explicit $1/stable assumption. A change is an accounting change in available inventory; Supply, Withdraw, Borrow, Repay, and other reserve operations must be reconciled before naming a transaction-level cause.
- sUSDe, sUSDS, scrvUSD: `depth_usd` is the sum of the exit-token-side inventory in configured secondary markets or a PSM. It is **not** a slippage-bounded executable quote, a vault redemption limit, or the entire venue's capacity. Pool swaps, LP additions/removals, PSM buffer management, market-set changes, and read failures can move it.

Exit semantics differ by venue. [Curve's scrvUSD vault design](https://github.com/curvefi/scrvusd) keeps underlying crvUSD idle and redeemable; the secondary-pool quote measures conversion of that crvUSD into USDT/USDC, not inability to redeem the vault share. For Aave, the [reserve-flag action table](https://aave.com/docs/mcp/safety) permits withdrawal when a reserve is frozen or a cap is reached, but not when paused; available underlying cash and a wallet's collateral health are separate constraints. Historical executable-state claims still require checking the deployed implementation at the relevant block.
- For every change card, use three distinct labels: **measured change** (accounting fact), **observed driver** (reconciled event/transaction class), and **possible leading signal** (time-ordered association). If no driver is reconciled, say “driver not yet attributed.”

## Preregistered simulation (before inspecting outcomes)

Unit: one venue × one observed snapshot. Do not mix backfilled and observed snapshots, or venue kinds in a single target. A valid row requires all enabled markets to have successful reads and the same market set at both ends. One anchored block per snapshot is required for transaction-level attribution; present pre-fix hourly results only as exploratory.

Target: whether the **same instrument** falls at least 20% by 24 hours after snapshot `t`; secondary target, 35% by seven days. Non-overlapping event starts and a 24-hour purge around train/test boundaries prevent adjacent hourly rows from inflating sample size. Features must exist by `t`, not be reconstructed from a future endpoint: lagged 24h capacity slope, pool skew and slope, Aave utilization/debt/available changes, prior 24h protocol net flow, contemporaneously published proposals/queued parameter changes, and trailing 24h realized asset volatility/depeg (once independently timestamped series are acquired). A proposal announced after `t` is not a feature.

Report episode counts, missingness, venue-stratified base rates, precision/recall, calibration, and event-study lead/lag plots. Compare with persistence/no-change and simple lagged-capacity baselines using rolling-origin holdouts; cluster uncertainty by non-overlapping episode, not hourly observation. **Promotion gate, set before running:** at least 20 independent target events and 20 independent controls, out-of-sample precision improvement of at least 10 percentage points over the best simple baseline, and a bootstrap 95% interval for improvement strictly above zero. Failing the gate means exploratory signal only, even if a chart looks compelling. Governance/volatility signal classes also require at least five independently timestamped exposures; otherwise report case studies, not a fitted model.

Simulation counterfactuals are mechanical sensitivity tests, not claims that an intervention would have caused the observed future: hold one pool reserve, Aave borrow/repay component, or PSM buffer fixed and recompute the instrument; stress a prior realized outflow/borrow shock over the recorded inventory. Never describe them as “what would have happened” without a validated behavioral model.

## Data/measurement hazards and repair

Before this pass the hourly collector captured a block number and then read individual fields at unpinned `latest`; a snapshot could straddle transactions. It also summed successful depth markets while a failed market contributed zero, so a failed RPC read could masquerade as a capacity collapse. The recorder now pins all state/depth reads to the captured block and emits `depth_usd: null` when any enabled market is incomplete, while retaining diagnostic market rows. This applies to **new** observations only. Historical event attribution is checked against ERC-20 transfer and pool event logs, but older before/after snapshots remain indicative rather than exact block-state matches.

`venue_flows` omits Aave Borrow/Repay and Curve swaps/LP actions, so it cannot itself explain the measured changes. Existing `venue_predictions` are 24h persistence bands, not driver models. Governance RSS/terms hashes are not a proposal-state feed. These gaps must remain visible in the product.

Evaluate chronological walk-forward holdouts against no-change, lagged quote trend, and lagged imbalance-only baselines.
