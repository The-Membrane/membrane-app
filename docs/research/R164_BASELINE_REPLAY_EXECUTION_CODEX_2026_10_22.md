The producer retains the original full horizon vector, path count, seed rule, pool order, and numerical sources.

Reconstruction acceptance alone does not prove historical prediction parity or original state identity.

The thin adapter authenticates the existing state reconstruction observer before importing it.

Every output, log, terminal receipt, sample receipt, and post-containment appendix must be fresh.
