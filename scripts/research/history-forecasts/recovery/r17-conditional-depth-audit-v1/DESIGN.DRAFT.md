## Exact issuance features and normalization

Producer Xbase columns are lsH,lsf,dm,wm,r24,r168,r720,dd720,rng24,lvol,btc_r24,btc_dm (`build_inputs.py:21,95–101,135–143`). Xshape columns are jump_share_D/W/M then semivariance_imbalance_D/W/M. Jump share is J/RV; imbalance is (downside semivariance−upside semivariance)/RV. Nonpositive RV makes these undefined. They use past completed bars, not future realized jumps.

lsH is log annualized forecast sigma, not log variance. Original normalization is `z_H = future_log_min / [exp(lsH)*sqrt(H/8760)]`: `a2_touch.py:111`, `a3_touch.py:375`, `a6_touch.py:639–654`. R17 stores lsH and lsf as float32 Xbase values. Exponentiating them reconstructs an approximate issued scale, not the original float64 scale/prediction identity. Event errors use stored raw-fH probabilities and original complete labels, not recomputed probabilities or rounded normalized-threshold decisions.

`fH_fallback` matters: producer lines120–124 can replace HAR predictions with the ser.sigma reference while lsH remains unchanged or unavailable. Primary baseline-matched normalization therefore uses effective logscale l*=lsf (column1) on fallback rows, otherwise lsH (column0). Require finite exp(l*)>0 and finite complete depth. Do not substitute arbitrary scales. Report fallback/nonfallback separately as metadata and preserve a HAR-only normalization sensitivity on finite lsH. Raw-probability availability does not imply HAR-scale availability. `p_fH_valid` and `issue_eligible` remain distinct. The historical fH library retains its original up-to-5%-missing-path tolerance, whereas primary evaluation labels require complete windows. This support mismatch remains a limitation, not silently repaired.

For complete labels with valid effective scale, retain signed standardized-depth count, mean, SD and fixed quantiles [.01,.05,.10,.25,.50,.75,.90,.95,.99], plus unstandardized log-depth quantiles. Preserve point mass/nonnegative depths. Shape descriptors include fixed tail-to-central interquantile-spacing ratios, e.g. (Q50−Q05)/(Q75−Q25) and (Q50−Q01)/(Q75−Q25), null when denominator<=0. These are empirical distribution summaries, not new fitted scale forecasts. Comparing raw depth, issued-scale-standardized depth and scale-invariant spacing ratios can assess compatibility with a pure multiplicative level pattern versus residual shape heterogeneity descriptively. Similar ratios do not prove a common law; different ratios can reflect mixed asset/calendar support or scale error. A fitted scale model may still be misspecified. Raw-fH error association does not establish pooling as its cause.

Retain unknown-label event-frequency bounds over all issued baseline-available origins: positives/N through (positives+unknown)/N. Report all-issued mean predicted probability beside those bounds. They are deterministic endpoint bounds, not missing-at-random estimates or confidence intervals. Scale-unavailable depth rows remain counted outside normalized summaries. No zero filling or imputation.
