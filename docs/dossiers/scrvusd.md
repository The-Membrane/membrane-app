## Q2 — Who can move the exit gate, how fast, with what notice?

**Chain fact:** no `cooldownDuration()` method — no on-chain cooldown gate on the vault.

**Official-docs fact (fetched 2026-09-06):** "No, your scrvUSD is never locked up. You
can take it out or swap it back to crvUSD whenever you want, without any waiting."
Withdrawal is instant/permissionless via the vault (Ethereum only). The docs state
Curve DAO governs crvUSD collateral/markets generally, but **no admin gate is described
for the scrvUSD exit itself.**
Source: `https://docs.curve.finance/user/yield/scrvusd`.

- **UNKNOWN — governance timelock / notice on any parameter that could affect exit**

## Q3 — Backing self-reference / attestation status?

**Composition facts (our snapshots):** the vault holds crvUSD; NAV ≈ 1.107 (Q1). Over
our coverage `totalAssets` moved from **19,487,977 crvUSD (~$19.49M, 2026-06-04)** to
**~$15.77M (2026-09-06)**. Observed size decline; no conclusion drawn.

**Backing of the underlying crvUSD (official docs, fetched 2026-09-06):** crvUSD is an
**overcollateralized CDP**, not an attestation-based instrument — "crvUSD is backed by
the assets provided as collateral," and "collateralized debt positions are managed
through arbitrage" (the LLAMMA soft-liquidation mechanism).
Sources: `https://docs.curve.finance/user/security/risks/crvusd`,
`https://docs.curve.finance/developer/crvusd/controller`.

- So backing is **on-chain collateral**, not an off-chain book — the opposite of the
  self-referential/opaque-composition shape in `worst-carry-venues.md` P3. Reported as
  the documented mechanism.
- **UNKNOWN — proof-of-reserves / attestation:** no PoR or attestation page was found
  in the official docs; whether one exists is UNKNOWN. (For a fully on-chain CDP, the
  collateral is directly readable on-chain, but we did not read the crvUSD
  controllers' collateral here.)

## Q4 — Yield source, and does the rate move?

**We do not record APY. UNKNOWN as a measured number.**

**Yield mechanism (official docs, fetched 2026-09-06):** "The extra crvUSD you earn
comes from fees paid by people who borrow crvUSD"; mechanistically, "the Vault receives
a dynamic percentage of the interest fees generated by the crvUSD from the FeeSplitter
contract." Variability: "The interest rate (or yield) for scrvUSD is variable and
adjusts regularly, typically every few days."
Sources: `https://docs.curve.finance/user/yield/scrvusd`,
`https://docs.curve.finance/developer/scrvusd/overview`.

- So per the official source the yield source is **crvUSD borrow-market interest**
  routed via FeeSplitter, and the rate **is variable**. Reported as documented
  mechanism, not a measurement.
- **UNKNOWN — the FeeSplitter percentage over time** and how it is governed; not read
  on-chain here.
