# Venue-capacity wide prospective cohort — preregistration draft

**Status: draft proposal, 2026-09-28. Not approved, enrolled, scheduled, deployed, or an alert.** This is a proposed new study and ledger, not an amendment to the frozen Sep 26–Oct 9 Core USDC/USDT calendar-control pilot. The latter remains a 14-day plumbing pilot with its original dates, missing days, source hashes, outcomes and seven/seven split. Even perfect capture yields only 28 market-days, whereas 20 disjoint events plus 20 disjoint controls require at least 40 units before incident clustering. Do not backfill, extend, or rescore the pilot as this cohort. The proposed study must receive an owner-approved, physically sealed protocol and working dry-run verifier **before** its first prospective feature or outcome read.

## Acquisition boundary and risk sets

Proposed enrollment is **12 consecutive calendar months** beginning at 00:00 UTC on a frozen start date `S`, with `S` no earlier than 2026-10-10 and no earlier than protocol approval, deployed-identity proof, and collector readiness. Freeze the exact `S`, end date `S + 12 calendar months`, contract registry, code/implementation identities, holder rule, q, cadence, feature definitions, budgets, split and file schema in the signed/sealed study manifest before enrollment. No rolling extension if events are scarce. Continue only already-scheduled final outcome follow-up for at most seven days beyond the acquisition end; that does not add anchors. A missed day remains missing, not moved to a later clock or filled from a historical RPC read.

The fixed primary assay is a **same-holder direct base-asset withdrawal of nominal 1,000,000 units** under an explicit $1/stable accounting assumption, not a guaranteed dollar value during depeg. Aave uses `Pool.withdraw(underlying, q, holder)` from the selected aToken holder; Compound III uses `Comet.withdraw(base, q)` from the selected base supplier. These are separate mechanisms and denominators. A successful pinned `eth_call` proves only isolated mechanical executability at that block, not signing control, transaction inclusion, simultaneous exits, or the cause of a later revert. Record a separate predeclared $10m sensitivity only if a pre-outcome manifest authorizes it with its own eligible risk set and score; never substitute a smaller q after an unfavorable outcome.

The Aave Ethereum registry is the previously frozen ordinary-supply set: **USDe, USDC, DAI, USDT, FRAX, USDS, LUSD, crvUSD, PYUSD, RLUSD**. The eight added reserves are exactly the ex-ante list and underlying/aToken pairs in [the parent study](venue-capacity-drivers.md#ex-ante-aave-stablecoin-reserve-expansion--frozen-before-added-reserve-outcomes-2026-09-25), not a new outcome-selected shortlist. Core USDC/USDT keep their existing identity rules. GHO is excluded from this ordinary-supply mechanism stratum because facilitator minting is not the same depositor-supply/cash process; a GHO study would require a new assay. The separate Compound III Ethereum stratum is the frozen **cUSDCv3, cUSDTv3, cUSDS** base markets from the five-market screen. Do not pool Aave and Compound events, controls, feature effects, or lift, and do not report a pooled market-level lift within either stratum. A future claim is market- and assay-specific; sparse markets remain inconclusive.

At each eligible day use the same **06:00 UTC** anchor clock. Start no earlier than 06:00 and no later than 06:30; complete the baseline witness and same-block feature set by 07:00. A day that misses either bound or fails finality, disk, source completeness, reserve/base identity, or verification is `missing`, not an adjusted capture. Freeze one finalized block/hash per day's market and retain capture start, each source's first local observation, feature completion, code/implementation hashes, asset/decimals, receipt physical SHA and verification status. A feature becomes usable only at its latest required local completion time, never at the earlier block or publisher timestamp. The full market-day ledger includes zero-witness, baseline-revert, ineligible, paused, RPC failure, pending and censored rows as well as successful rows.

For holder discovery, freeze one bounded, deterministic **prior-2,000-finalized-block, top-100 recipient** rule before `S`: rank by the predeclared on-chain receipt/transfer order and address tie-break, exclude zero/dead/low precompile sentinels and code-bearing addresses, and require a same-block claim of at least q plus an exact-q baseline call success. Select the first eligible address as the **one primary witness per market-day**; preserve all 100 candidates and reasons, including no candidate, without replacing the selected holder after any subsequent outcome. This is a bounded recent-recipient witness, not a holder census or random population sample. Require deployed Pool/Comet proxy and implementation proof, reserve/base and aToken identity, exact calldata, holder balance, allowance/health or pause context as applicable, and hash-pinned reads at every observation. An empty-code address is not proof of key control. If source/identity proof cannot be completed, mark the market-day unavailable; do not relabel its result as a clean executable event or control.

## Outcome clock: sampled onset, not interpolated onset

Predeclare two separately scored horizons: primary **24 hours**, secondary **seven days** after the frozen anchor block timestamp, with consensus-finalized target blocks. For each baseline-successful fixed holder/q, propose an exact-call outcome sample at every elapsed hour through +24h, then every six elapsed hours through +7d, using the first finalized block at or after each target. Freeze a bounded grace window and RPC/disk budget before `S`; a late or failed target stays missing/censored and is never silently replaced by a convenient neighboring block. Every sample records the actual target time, block/hash, first local read time, exact holder/q, result class, remaining claim and deployed identity. Success, clean attributable execution revert, preexisting revert, holder attrition, permission/pause change, code drift, ambiguous revert, and provider error are distinct. An isolated 24h or seven-day endpoint cannot establish when a failure began.

For a baseline-successful path, the **first observed failure** has true onset in the open/closed interval `(last observed success, first observed failure]`, provided there is no missing scheduled sample in between; with a gap, retain a wider interval and mark it unresolved. Let `T_signal` be the latest local time at which the complete pre-outcome signal was available. A ≥6h **true-onset lower-bound lead** is established only if a verified **post-signal successful** outcome sample uses a finalized block whose timestamp is at least six hours after `T_signal`, is read locally after that block, and precedes the first clean failure, with an otherwise complete interval and stable identity/holder claim. Then onset must occur after that successful block. A success read six hours later from an _earlier_ block does not qualify. If the first failure follows the anchor with no qualifying post-signal success, or samples are missing/ambiguous, the onset is interval-censored and the ≥6h lead gate is **false**, regardless of the distance to the first failure sample. Count distinct incident onsets, not every later failing hourly call. Report both feature local time and outcome block/local-read times and their offsets; never infer availability from a block timestamp alone.

A full-window **sampled-success control** is a baseline-successful primary witness with every scheduled, identity-valid sample through the chosen horizon succeeding and no missing/ambiguous sample. It is not a claim of continuous availability between samples. A no-witness, missing day, pause, attrition or partial-window success is not a quiet control. Retain all unflagged and flagged eligible market-days for precision denominators; do not manufacture a balanced case-control sample and then call its precision a deployment estimate. Improvements and recoveries are recorded but do not erase a prior event.

## Signals, split, and independence

Version and locally timestamp the candidate lanes before outcomes: same-block cash/q and exact pre-anchor cash slope; Aave borrow/repay/supply/withdraw and Compound base-flow reconcilers; peg, pool imbalance, liquidity and realized volatility; PSM/queue buffer and allocation actions where the route actually uses them; peer-rate spread; and published, created, queued, executable, cancelled and executed governance stages linked to a deployed target. Each lane requires bounded complete source coverage, raw evidence and first local availability. A proposal or permission is not an executable-exit impairment. Missing, after-anchor, post-outcome, unlinked, or reconstructed-after-the-fact features abstain. Freeze feature transforms and any candidate thresholds on development data only; compare ablations to persistence/no-change, lagged capacity and the **best simple contemporaneously knowable cash/q plus pre-B cash-slope baseline**. Do not call a correlation causal or tune a threshold on the holdout.

The **last four calendar months** of `S` to `S+12 months` are the untouched chronological holdout; the earlier eight months are development. Exclude anchors within **seven days on each side** of the split boundary from independent training/test evaluation, and exclude any anchor whose seven-day outcome window crosses it. The same embargo applies to any later model-selection boundary. Freeze the selected model/threshold and source SHA before holdout scoring. Cluster repeated days, holders, markets sharing the same transaction or reserve event, and common calendar stress into incident groups; conservatively join overlapping seven-day windows and report both raw market-days and effective independent groups. A holder reappearing daily is not a new independent event. Prespecify same-market, same-q full-window sampled-success controls with matching calendar exposure; keep zero-witness and failed-coverage denominators visible. Report Aave and Compound separately and per-market, never rescue a weak market with another market's onsets.

The **minimum promotion gates for each market-specific, executable-exit claim** are all of: at least **20 independent clean deterioration events and 20 independent full-window sampled-success controls** in the untouched holdout; a qualified ≥6h true-onset lower-bound lead; holdout precision **≥80%** and event recall **≥50%**; precision improvement **≥10 percentage points** over the best frozen simple baseline; and a **cluster/bootstrap 95% interval for that improvement strictly above zero**. Report calibration, false notices per market-week, recall/precision denominators, actual lead distribution, missingness and censoring. Governance and volatility candidate classes additionally require **≥5 independently timestamped exposures** with available pre-outcome sources; fewer permit case studies only. These gates combine the parent study's promotion rule and fixed-holder validation floor; satisfying a cash-proxy onset count does not satisfy the executable-event count. No cross-market pooled lift or pooled alert is allowed.

## Existing knowledge, activation, and stop rule

This is **not a pristine historical study**. The earlier five-market cash/pause screen, eight-reserve expansion, cUSDS holder replays and their outcomes, Sep 26 selected-event and calendar Core baselines, some +6h/+24h outcomes, and subsequent as-of audits have already been inspected. The expanded historical $1m set had 35 market-level **proxy** onsets but only 26 exploratory calendar-linked groups; five cUSDS coarse-grid candidate crossings were already non-executable at B−1. Those observations can motivate instrument design and baseline choices but cannot be counted as this prospective cohort's holdout events, controls, warning lead, or untouched calibration. The Sep 26 calendar outcome rows with unresolved deployed implementation remain censored, not clean successes. Do not retune this proposed protocol using later pilot outcomes after freeze.

Before any `--run` or scheduler installation, owner and independent reviewer must approve a versioned manifest, the exact 12-month start/end, market/route and code registry, q and holder rule, hourly/six-hour outcome sampling feasibility, budget and disk floor, source and clock verifiers, chronological split, clustering and scorer. This memo proposes those choices; **it does not prove the collector exists or that twelve months will yield enough independent incidents**. Test dry-run failure and no-overwrite behavior before enrollment. Stop on resource, finality, identity or coverage failure, retain the last verified immutable receipt, and record the scheduled slot missing; never backfill it as prospective warning evidence. At the approved end, publish the full missing/censored ledger and gate failures even if zero events occur. Until every market-specific gate passes on the untouched holdout, the product remains observation-only: **no predictive badge or user alert**.
