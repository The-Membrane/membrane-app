## Factual support and counters

The helper authenticates the original scope, accepted independent fit and descriptive evidence, raw/merged pins, sources/runtime, and original twenty fit receipts. Original shared NumPy RNG replay checks every recorded membership and accounts for train sampling, validation sampling, per-model prefixes and permutations per recorded epoch. Membership is checked before accepting feature counters.

All five models (`nmlp`, `ngru`, `ntcn`, `ntfm`, `nmlp0`), four original retrospective hash/time cells, three roles (train, validation, test), and three horizons (1/3/12 hours) are retained: **180 strata**, including zero support. These roles reuse observations across models and cells; their counts are membership exposures, neither independent samples nor epoch-weighted examples. Training prefixes retain original caps 60k/20k/30k/20k/60k; validation retains the original at-most-60k sample; tests retain original daily origins. Early-time cells use future peer-series training support.

Each canonical series is staged once by the original `series_arrays` call; loader returns are captured without changing them. Separate pre-fill/pre-clip formulas must reproduce original series and batch array descriptors exactly, including dtype and bytes, before counters are accepted. This does not claim each underlying input file is physically read once: original loaders can reread price/volume sources. Unique selected rows are processed in batches of at most 1,024, then distributed to reused role strata. Actual windows are HW=48 and DW=30, with 12 static fields, two gap fields and three horizon one-hot fields.

Per field, exact integer counters are denominator, rows, invalid, fallback, strictly_clipped_low, strictly_clipped_high, equal_low_boundary, equal_high_boundary, and rows_affected. Element fractions derive count/denominator; affected-row fraction derives rows_affected/rows; zero denominators imply undefined/null fractions. Invalid/fallback masks come from genuine pre-transform values, not sanitized zero or equality at a clip boundary. Strict clipping uses `<`/`>`; boundary equality is separate and does not itself count as an affected row. Volume clipping is +/-5, normalized sequence clipping +/-10, and static clipping +/-20. Original ratio-history all-invalid masks are distinguished from rolling history after volume fallback to zero.
