# Direct supplier settled payout context

`GET /api/carry/direct-supplier-flow-context?marketKey=<key>` reads only the local sealed public-receipt archive. It is available in development on the loopback interface and does not use Neon or make an RPC request. Supported frozen direct market keys are `aaveV3Usdc`, `aaveV3Usde`, `sparkLendUsdt`, and `compoundV3Usdc`.

The reader selects the newest contiguous suffix within a 1,000-segment and 64 MiB read budget, then verifies each selected segment's file binding, paired logs and receipts, SHA seal, numeric range continuity, and adjacent boundary hashes and timestamps. Once the archive exceeds that budget, counts and maxima refer only to the reported coverage interval; `historyScope` says `bounded_contiguous_recent_suffix`. It does not independently verify every intermediate block header. A missing archive returns `no_sealed_segments`; an older acquisition gap cuts the selected suffix at that gap, while malformed, tampered, or overlapping selected segments fail closed with HTTP 503. The API never returns holder or receiver addresses or exact user request amounts. It returns:

- the exact frozen route and underlying identity, observed finalized coverage interval, and settled payout count;
- the count of settled withdrawals whose receipt holder and underlying receiver are the same address, plus the largest such single payout in raw underlying units;
- the exact maximum gross withdrawal across any complete observed 24-hour window, with that window's start and end timestamps. Less than 24 hours of contiguous coverage returns `coverage_under_24h` instead of a partial maximum or zero.

At the October 1 local replay, Aave USDe had 21.23 hours of contiguous coverage and therefore no 24-hour maximum. Aave USDC and Spark USDT had complete 24-hour windows. Compound v3 USDC completed its first pinned historical target with **133 contiguous sealed segments through block 26,088,300** (64 blocks each except the final target slice), covering 28.29 hours from block 26,079,847. The loopback API replay found 23 settled same-address supplier payouts and 58 corroborated but unclassified Comet Withdraw events. Across its observed complete 24-hour windows, the largest verified gross Comet event volume was **4,313,068.406243 USDC from 68 events**. A Comet Withdraw can include borrowing or mixed flows; [Compound's Comet specification](https://github.com/compound-finance/comet/blob/main/SPEC.md) defines base withdrawal as an outgoing base-token transfer that may cross from supplier balance into borrowing. The classifier does not turn those events into supplier payouts. The API reports `unclassifiedWithdrawalCount`, `coverage.receiptsComplete=false`, and the combined observed Comet Withdraw event amount. It withholds the settled-supplier 24-hour maximum because of those unclassified events; the separately labeled gross Comet event maximum is historical market outflow, not holder-executable exit capacity or a forecast. These observations will change as the native recorder appends new segments.

These are historical public payouts, including other holders. Same-address receipt pairing is a stronger settled first-leg observation than an exit simulation, but it does not prove a queried holder's future exit, a complete multi-leg payout route, or available capacity. Paired RPC responses and local hashes establish replay consistency; they are not independent proof that upstream providers are honest. The current observed window can lag the finalized chain head if backfill is behind.

`GET /api/carry/direct-supplier-supply-context?marketKey=<key>` separately replays receipt-matched underlying `Supply` transfers for the same four markets. It returns observed gross underlying inflow, event count, and a complete 24-hour maximum only when the newest contiguous finalized suffix covers at least 24 hours. On October 1 the local suffixes covered 10.47 hours (Aave USDC, Spark USDT) and 10.78 hours (Aave USDe, Compound USDC), so every maximum correctly reports `coverage_under_24h`. The underlying inflow is **not** net cash replenishment: a Compound Comet Supply can repay debt, and no Supply event alone determines how much the next holder can withdraw. This local endpoint is read-only, development loopback only, and independent of the withdrawal endpoint's availability.
