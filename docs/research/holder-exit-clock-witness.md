# Holder exit clock witness contract

**Status, 2026-10-04:** an offline token verifier and an opt-in trust-material capture helper exist. No holder artifact has been submitted to an external timestamp service or verified against an independently authenticated root. No local clock is eligible for `independently_witnessed_utc`.

The [validation risk set](../../scripts/research/holder-exit-validation-risk-set.mjs) currently requires independently witnessed feature, issue, and score clocks. Direct gross-flow features additionally require cryptographically verified source-to-baseline block ancestry. The local publication receipts and two-host parent-link tuples are useful audit evidence but satisfy neither stronger claim.

## Chronology to prove

```text
complete feature bytes ── external timestamp ── issue bytes binding feature SHA
                                              └── external timestamp before outcome
outcome block and exact holder assay ── score bytes ── external timestamp by deadline
```

For each exact artifact, retain its bytes, SHA-256, RFC 3161 request DER with nonce, response DER, signer chain, and a separately authenticated trust root. Offline replay must verify response status, TSA signature and certificate purpose, request nonce, SHA-256 message imprint against the actual bytes, policy, UTC `genTime` and accuracy. Order witnesses conservatively when their accuracy intervals overlap. Issuance is no earlier than the issue token's verified time. Tokens acquired only after an outcome cannot establish prospective issuance. The official [RFC 3161 standard](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3161), [OpenSSL verification interface](https://docs.openssl.org/3.5/man1/openssl-ts/), and [Sigstore TSA documentation](https://github.com/sigstore/timestamp-authority/blob/main/README.md) specify these objects; trust material must be independently pinned or obtained through [Sigstore's trusted-root distribution](https://docs.sigstore.dev/cosign/system_config/custom_components/).

The witness must hash the **exact final file bytes**, including a trailing newline if present. A ledger's internal `sha256` field may hash its body before that seal and is not interchangeable with the raw-file digest. The witness sidecar may identify file paths, but it cannot choose its own trusted root. An offline verifier may first report only `verified_under_supplied_pin`; the validation risk set must not accept that as an independent clock until root provenance and the artifact binding are independently checked. For chronology, require the feature's signed upper time bound to precede the issue's signed lower bound; require the issue's upper bound to precede the earliest defensible outcome time, and the score's upper bound to meet its deadline. Overlapping accuracy intervals abstain.

The [offline RFC 3161 verifier](../../scripts/research/holder-exit-rfc3161-witness.mjs) now checks the exact artifact imprint, nonce, caller-pinned policy, SHA-256 CMS signing digest, certificate chain and purpose, and the token's UTC accuracy interval. It reports only `verified_under_supplied_pin`. The [Sigstore trust-material helper](../../scripts/research/sigstore-tsa-trust-snapshot.mjs) captured a 38,639-byte [public trust candidate](../../data/research/venue-signals/sigstore-tsa-trust-v1/candidate.json) on 2026-10-04T22:21:39Z; its retained metadata and target hashes match, and its TSA certificate fingerprints are recorded in that file. The candidate still says `offlineTufReplay:false` and `rootProvenanceGateSatisfied:false`. No holder token has been submitted or verified, and the validation gate has not advanced.

A valid TSA token attests that a digest existed by signed UTC. It does not prove that another party could retrieve the raw artifact. If a public commitment is required, first verify the signed digest entry, checkpoint, and inclusion proof in a transparency log, then timestamp a canonical bundle containing that proof and artifact SHA. [Rekor v2](https://github.com/sigstore/architecture-docs/blob/main/rekor-v2-spec.md) supplies inclusion evidence but no timestamp of its own. A self-recorded readback is operational evidence, not an independent witness of raw-byte availability; that stronger claim needs an external archiver to fetch and attest the exact bytes.

Do not promote a string label or a locally generated receipt into the validation gate. Wire a verifier that reopens each artifact and its witness, checks chronological order, and emits a typed proof result before the panel may use `independently_witnessed_utc`. Keep externally witnessed **digest existence**, public digest commitment, and raw-byte availability as separate claims. Physical independence of holder episodes, actual impairment/recovery outcomes, and untouched prospective calibration remain separate requirements.
