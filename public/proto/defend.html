Defend

Design a better risk curator.

Submit a program. Each step it reads market state — reserves, oracle price, utilization, clearable depth, flow, position distribution — and outputs the whole risk vector: liquidation LTV, borrow gap, collateral caps, delay length, oracle tolerance. We score it over 1,000 randomized simulations against hidden regimes it cannot see, and rank it by average edge. Win on-chain and your policy becomes protocol infrastructure — public, forkable, your name on it. Run a private model offchain and charge for it: compute costs are the only real fee moat, because anything fully on-chain can be copied the day it wins. The house entries below are real venue policies; the challenge is beating Membrane’s own algorithm.

What the sim cannot see

●Venue recalls are not modeled. Live positions can pull deployed capital back to repay debt before collateral sells. The sim has no such lever, so live outcomes should beat sim outcomes in shallow breaches. If they do not, that is signal.
●Sim edge is not live edge. Calibration drifts from the moment it is measured. The vault chart above is the correction, updated as live data settles.
●Rebreach timers are simulated both ways — with and without reset — because the shipping behavior is an open decision. Your score shows both columns.
