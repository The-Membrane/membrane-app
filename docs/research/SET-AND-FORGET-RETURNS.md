# Set-and-forget returns: what the position earned and paid

The return half of the set-and-forget sim (board Q20; owner direction 2026-10-11, "historical return statements"). The survival half is [SET-AND-FORGET-LTV.md](SET-AND-FORGET-LTV.md). Generated by `scripts/position-sim/set-and-forget-sim.ts --returns` on 2026-10-12. Every number below is in `public/data/price-history/set-and-forget-returns.json`, the copy the UI serves. The canonical file and the rate history behind it are in the private datasets repo (board rule N18): `membrane-datasets/set-and-forget-returns/` and `membrane-datasets/net-apy-history/`. **Historical replay over a named period, with n stated. Not a forecast, not a probability.**

## The position

The A1 copy claim's position, one size:

- ETH, delayed class, line 80%, opened at **HF 2 (40% LTV)**.
- **$100,000 of collateral, $40,000 of debt.**
- **Carry:** the whole debt is deployed at Aave USDC. Membrane recalls it under each named exit capacity (cash vs book at $10M / $50M / $250M, typical and bad; the everyone-exits floor).
- **Levered long:** the debt is swapped back into ETH. Nothing is deployed and nothing is recalled.
- **Incumbent:** the same collateral and the same $40,000 debt on Aave v3 (WETH collateral, USDC debt). For carry, the borrowed USDC is supplied to Aave USDC.
- Left alone for 30, 90 or 365 days from the close of an hour. Every start hour, stride 1h.

## Lines

| Line | Membrane | Aave v3 |
|---|---|---|
| Yield earned | Venue supply APR × the debt still deployed. Priced at Membrane's whole book at the venue: the case's book; the floor names none, so the default $50M book. Gross, before Membrane's curator-set share, so an upper bound on what reaches the user (†). | Venue supply APR × the deployed debt, at the position's own size. Nothing is recalled. |
| Borrow cost | **Unavailable.** Carry: deployed debt accrues 0%, and carry debt never exceeds the capital still deployed (a recall lowers both, a sale lowers the debt). The cost is a curator-set share of the venue yield, not fixed pre-launch, so it is never priced. Levered long: all its debt is undeployed and pays a variable rate that follows the avoidance rate (owner 2026-10-12, board Q24). That rate has no history pre-launch. | Measured hourly variable borrow APR × the debt still owed. |
| Collateral sold | The stress engine's sales (`exposedUsd`). Fees omitted, so understated. | Seized collateral, including the liquidation bonus. |
| Yield given up | What the capital the recalls drew would have earned for the rest of the window. | 0 by construction: a liquidation is paid from collateral, the deployed USDC stays. |

## Sources

- **Venue yield: the net-APY history** (`membrane-datasets/net-apy-history/aave-v3-usdc-1h.json`, private). Built by `scripts/position-sim/build-net-apy-history.ts` with lib/netApy's own pinned-block reader (`readVenueSnapshot` + `ratesAtSize`, PR #92), one archive read per UTC hour at the block `public/data/venue-stress/history.json` read for that hour. Each sample prices the supply APR with no added deposit and with each book added.
- **It starts on 2024-07-27 16:00 UTC (block 20,398,939).** lib/netApy reads Aave v3 through `getVirtualUnderlyingBalance` and `getInterestRateDataBps`, which exist only from Aave v3.1. The read one hour earlier (block 20,398,640) reverts. A window that opens before the history starts gets **no yield and no statement**: it is counted as unavailable, never priced.
- **Cross-check.** lib/netApy's borrow APR equals the venue-stress hourly borrow APR at every shared block to within 0.005 percentage points (the venue-stress file rounds to 0.01).
- **Hourly, on purpose.** A first build sampled every 6 h. Its grid landed on 00:00 UTC each day, the hour of Aave USDC's daily drain, and held a supply APR near 55% for 6 h where the hourly borrow APR averaged about 15%. It overstated the 90-day median yield by about 8%. The published history is hourly (19,234 samples, none failed), and a test fails on a coarser one.
- **Aave borrow APR:** `public/data/venue-stress/history.json`, `aave-core-usdc` `apr`, hourly archive reads from 2023-10-01.
- **Aave liquidation parameters:** Aave v3 WETH liquidation threshold and bonus, read in the same archive call as each net-APY sample. They were 83% and 5% for the whole period. Each liquidation repays 57.76% of the debt, the median of 3,111 measured Oct-10-2025 Aave liquidations (`public/data/oct10-2025/protocols.json`).
- **Price path:** the survival table's (`eth-usd-1h.json`, Chainlink ETH/USD), as a replay of each hour's oracle low then its close.

What the table says:

- **No sale where the venue covered most of the book.** ETH carry at HF 2 with the debt at Aave USDC sold no collateral in any 30, 90 or 365-day window of the period at ×0.8125 or more (the $10M and $50M books, typical and bad; the $250M book, typical). Aave v3 sold collateral in 867 of the 17,052 90-day windows and 4,246 of the 10,452 365-day windows, up to $34,506 and $40,663 of collateral (bonus included).
- **Recall instead of sale costs yield, and the cost was small here.** Recall drew deployed capital in 1,075 of the 90-day windows at the $50M book (up to $10,309 of the $40,000). The yield that capital gave up was at most $14 over 90 days and $377 over 365 days. At the median it was $0: in most windows nothing crossed the line.
- **Thin exit capacity sells, as in the survival table.** At the floor (everyone exits at once) a carry position sold in 355 (typical) and 808 (bad) of the 90-day windows. At the $250M book in its bad event (×0.1806) it sold in 8.
- **Membrane's book lowers the rate it earns.** Over 90 days the median yield on $40,000 was $369 at the $10M book, $354 at $50M and $310 at $250M, against $377 for the same $40,000 on Aave (no book). The book is added to the venue's supply at each hour (lib/netApy `ratesAtSize`).
- **Aave charged more than it paid.** Borrowing USDC on Aave and supplying it to Aave USDC had a negative net carry in every 30 and 90-day window: median −$43 and −$131 on $40,000. It was positive only in 365-day windows where a liquidation repaid part of the debt with collateral while the $40,000 stayed deployed (supply APR ≤ borrow APR at every one of the 19,234 hours). Membrane's matching cost is the curator-set share of the yield, which this replay does not price.
- **The line matters for a levered long.** At the same 40% start LTV, the Membrane levered long (line 80%) sold in 917 of the 90-day windows, Aave (threshold 83%) in 867. Over 365 days: 4,243 against 4,246.
- **One period.** Every window opens 2024-07-27 or later. It contains one large drawdown: the Chainlink ETH/USD close was $4,085 on 2024-12-16 and its hourly low $1,395 on 2025-04-09 (`eth-usd-1h.json`). The 365-day windows open no later than 2025-10-06. Nothing here describes 2020–2024.

## Not modelled

- Membrane's curator-set share of the yield. It is never priced pre-launch.
- Membrane's liquidation fee and the fee on a recall cure (LiquidationEngine STEP 2.5). The Membrane "sold" figure is understated.
- Interest accrual on the debt and compounding of the yield (simple interest on the balance).
- Gas, slippage, MEV, and incentives (Merkl).
- Aave parameter changes inside a window (held at the window's start; none occurred in the period).
- An Aave liquidation needs a liquidator in that half hour. It is modelled as always present.

## What this means for copy

- **State the period and n every time.** Example: "Opened at HF 2 any hour from 2024-07-27 to 2026-07-08 and held 90 days (n = 17,052): …". Never extend a figure to 2020–2024. Before 2024-07-27 the yield is unavailable.
- **History, not odds.** Report what windows earned and sold as quantiles with n. Do not turn window counts into a chance.
- **Open owner decision: a bound for the levered long's rate.** Q24 bounds the avoidance rate by the venue supply APR in its stress axis. With that bound, the levered long's Membrane borrow cost would be at most the venue supply yield on its debt: about $377 median over 90 days on $40,000 (the Aave yield of the carry rows), less after a sale. This memo does not print that bound without an owner ruling.
- **Never promise positive carry.** Membrane's borrow cost is a curator-set share of the yield and is not priced here. The yield line is the gross venue yield, an upper bound on what reaches the user.
- **Name the book.** Membrane's whole book at Aave USDC lowers the rate it earns: the $250M book earned less than the $10M book in the same windows.
- **Say the line.** At the same 40% start LTV, a levered long on Membrane (line 80%) can sell in more windows than on Aave (threshold 83%).
