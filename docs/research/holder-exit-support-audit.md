# Prospective holder exit support audit

Run `NODE_OPTIONS='--max-old-space-size=384' node scripts/research/holder-exit-support-audit.mjs` from the app repository. The audit reads verified Morpho V2, direct Aave/Spark, Aave USDC frozen-Q V2, Compound v3 USDC, sGHO V1 and fixed 1 GHO V2, sUSDS, stUSDS, USD3, and all three Fluid fToken route-specific issue and score chains against the frozen 25-group/67-subject manifest. It does not call a provider or write a ledger. Its output has no holder addresses. `groups[].withIssue` is coverage in these audited route lanes, not a claim that another holder assay has no issue for that route.

The accounting unit is a distinct issue record at one exact route, destination, original asset, raw exit amount, and horizon. Multiple amount labels or horizons from one issue are never separate issue episodes for that cell. Distinct issue records are **not certified statistically independent**: the same holder or market shock can recur. `distinctHolders` is reported per cell as another support diagnostic. Baseline failures, successful controls, recoveries, still impaired observations, new reversions, ambiguous outcomes, holder attrition, missed capture windows, and pending or missing scores have separate counts. An Aave/Spark baseline revert is impaired only if verified holder coverage meets the frozen Q and `coveredRevert` is true; a larger Q than the holder can cover is unavailable. Direct V1, Compound, and sGHO V1/sUSDS impaired baselines are ineligible for follow-up scoring and appear as `unscoredByDesign`. Aave USDC frozen-Q V2, stUSDS/USD3, and sGHO fixed-Q V2 can score validator-confirmed impaired baselines.

Compound is a separate supplied-position mechanism. A successful Comet `withdraw` counts as a baseline control only when the verified holder supplied balance covers the original raw USDC amount; otherwise the call might borrow and is baseline-unavailable. A baseline revert counts as impaired only when the validated measurement confirms both full holder coverage and `coveredRevert: true`. The Compound diagnostic separates insufficient-coverage baselines and target success, covered revert with unknown cause, holder attrition, missed capture, baseline ineligibility, pending, and missing. Its score validator measures only supplied-success baselines, so a covered baseline revert is currently `unscoredByDesign`, not a duration sample. Scores remain read-only simulations, not mined payouts.

Each sGHO V2 issue links to one verified V1 parent and replays the exact 1 GHO amount at the parent's baseline block. If V1 measured that same holder, amount, and baseline block, its `baselineStatus` must agree with V2 or the audit rejects the chain. A V1 unavailable case does not assert a conflicting status. Where that parent also has a V1 1 GHO case at the same horizon, the audit counts **only V2** in that exact cell and reports the excluded V1 count as `support.correlatedSghoV1ObservationsExcluded`. Other V1 amounts remain in their own exact cells. V1 and V2 are correlated by holder and market event; even the retained cells cannot be treated as independent population samples. V2 scores distinguish measured recovery, still reverting, lost exitability, holder attrition, censored capture, pending, and missing.

Each Aave USDC frozen-Q V2 issue links to a fresh verified V1 parent before its first target. It freezes every parent case whose exact raw Q has a verified success or a verified covered revert with enough holder aTokens; uncovered V1 reverts remain unavailable. The sidecar keeps the parent's horizon times and scores the **same holder and raw Q** at each first-finalized target with two origin receipts and replay. Later success after covered baseline revert is a simulated recovery; continued covered revert, new restriction, and holder attrition remain separate. Its native sweep rotates the first active target across ten-minute buckets, reserves an overdue censor slot, and tries at most two independent origin pairs per target per tick so repeated early replays do not monopolize later due targets. Correlated V1 cells for those parent cases are excluded and counted by `support.correlatedAaveV1ObservationsExcluded`. Old V1 episodes are not backdated into prospective V2 issues. The local clock and read-only `eth_call` do not prove mined USDC delivery or calibrated duration.

Fluid fToken baseline `withdraw` success is a control; a baseline EVM revert is **unassessed**, not a proven liquidity impairment. The `fluid` diagnostic on each Fluid cell separates baseline generic reverts and target covered success/revert, holder ineligibility, entitlement uncertainty, identity change, missed capture, pending, and missing score. A covered target requires the same holder to hold enough shares for the original asset amount. A covered target revert remains ambiguous as to cause. A later simulated success is a same-holder read-only call, never a mined payout.

At the 2026-10-01 23:50 UTC local read, these verified chains had measured baselines for **28/67 exact subjects in 15/25 groups**; **39 subjects and 10 groups** lacked an issue in these audited lanes. There were **2,970 exact amount/horizon cells**, of which **1,498 had a measured baseline** after strict direct-market coverage checks. One Aave USDC V2 issue was sealed, with its H1 target still pending. **Zero** cells had at least 20 distinct impaired and 20 distinct control holders, and there was no observed Aave V2 recovery yet. The direct ledger also includes supplemental Aave USDe outside the frozen denominator; the audit reports such issues only as `outOfCohortIssues`. Re-run the audit for current counts because native recorders continue to append.

This is a support diagnostic, not an estimated failure probability, likely restriction duration, or indication that a chosen holder could exit. The ledger's local operator issue clock also lacks an independently witnessed first-known timestamp. A future model must resolve that limitation and pass prospective untouched holdout and censoring gates before publishing a holder forecast.
