# scrvUSD NOW-origin expected slots — local research plan

`node scripts/research/scrvusd-now-schedule.mjs --draft <plannedAtUtc> <startAtUtc> <endAtUtc>` prints a canonical JSON manifest. All timestamps are explicit UTC with milliseconds. The half-open slots align to UTC hours, the first starts at least two hours after the caller's local planning time, and the window is at most 14 days. The fixed arms are 1h, 2h, 24h, and 7d for the existing 1,000 crvUSD holder and direct ERC-4626 withdrawal route. The command does not save or publish anything. `--verify <manifest.json>` validates the parsed canonical object and its local SHA seal; it does not authenticate the file's physical bytes or creation time.

`auditNowSchedule(manifest, {asOfUtc, verifiedIssueRows, boundAttemptRows})` is a pure read-only audit. The caller must supply complete, externally verified issue rows and attempt rows. It lists every due slot and every arm as `missing`, `unbound`, `issued`, `abstained`, `failed`, or `unknown`; a slot becomes due at its exclusive close. No attempt row means `missing`, even if an old issue timestamp falls inside the slot. An `issued` operational state requires exact manifest and slot references on both the attempt and linked verified issue. A claimed attempt linked to an old issue without issue-side binding is `unbound` and contributes no issued coverage. Existing v1 NOW-origin issues do not carry `scheduleBinding` and therefore remain unbound. `boundIssueCount`, `unboundIssueCount`, and `unconfirmedBoundIssueCount` count only issues inside **due** slots; ambiguous duplicate attempts enter the last group, not issued coverage. Pre-plan and post-plan issues are counted as `outsidePlanIssueCount`, and issues in open future slots as `notYetDueSlotIssueCount`. A wrong manifest, slot, arm, linked issue, or half-open time fails closed; multiple attempt rows for one cell are `unknown`.

This is **local research only**. The printed seal and caller-supplied `plannedAtUtc` are self-attestations, not proof the plan was committed before a slot. There is no bound-attempt producer, independent timestamp, database publication or confirmation, scheduled run, calibration, probability, likely duration, or user alert. The current v1 issuer and verifier cannot emit or accept an issue with `scheduleBinding`; an `issued` state is a hand-built future-schema test scenario, not a live attainable state. `prospectiveScheduleConfirmed` and `calibratedForecastEligible` stay false even for that hypothetical operational row. Real prospective coverage requires an independently persisted manifest before the first slot and immutable, verified, exact-bound attempt and issue records; retroactive timestamp matching cannot provide that evidence.
